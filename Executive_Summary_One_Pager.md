## The Challenge
Our Databricks infrastructure costs peaked at **$23,149/month** in August 2025 due to:
- Over-provisioned always-on pool clusters
- Inconsistent VM sizing across environments  
- Single driver bottleneck causing resource contention

---

## The Solution: 3-Phase Optimization

| Phase | When | What We Did | Result |
|:-----:|:----:|-------------|:------:|
| **1** | Aug | Removed pool clusters → On-demand compute | **-14%** |
| **2** | Sep | Right-sized to D4s_v3 (3-5 workers) + 100% utilization | **-62%** |
| **3** | Oct-Nov | Task group clustering with dedicated clusters | **-31%** |
